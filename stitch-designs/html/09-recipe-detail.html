Lemon Ricotta Pancakes

Cloud-like textures meet the bright, zesty notes of Meyer lemons. These are the definitive weekend brunch pancakes—elegant, effortless, and impossibly tender.

skillet Cook 20m

Ingredients

Preparation

01

Whisk Dry Ingredients

In a large bowl, whisk together flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt. Make a well in the center to keep the batter light and airy.

02

Combine the Wet Base

Whisk the ricotta, milk, egg yolks, lemon juice, zest, and vanilla in a separate medium bowl until smooth. The ricotta should be creamy and well-integrated.

03

The "Secret" Whipped Whites

Beat the egg whites until stiff peaks form. Fold them gently into the main batter at the very end to ensure that signature cloud-like lift.
